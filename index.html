Our Story

From Tuscany to Your Table

Founded in 1995 by Chef Marco Rossini, Bella Vista brings the authentic flavors of Tuscany to your neighborhood. Our commitment to traditional cooking methods and locally-sourced ingredients creates an unforgettable dining experience.

Our Culinary Philosophy

We believe in simplicity and quality. Every dish is prepared with hand-selected ingredients, many sourced from local farms and our own herb garden. Our pasta is made fresh daily using traditional Italian techniques passed down through generations.

Fresh & Local

Our menu changes seasonally to showcase the best local produce. From San Marzano tomatoes to locally-raised organic meats, we partner with trusted suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.
