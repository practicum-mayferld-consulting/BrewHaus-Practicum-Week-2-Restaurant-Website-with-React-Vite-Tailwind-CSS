At Savoria, cooking is not performance—it is communion. We celebrate the tension between classic French rigor and California's wild coastal bounty. Each day begins with baskets brought directly from Sebastopol soil and concludes beside our open white-oak hearth.

Our kitchen rejects industrial shortcuts. Sauces simmer undisturbed for thirty-six hours; butter is cultured in-house with sea salts hand-harvested along Big Sur. Here, dining returns to its purest form: unhurried, multisensory, and profound.

"True culinary luxury isn't excess—it is the reverence for pure, unadulterated ingredients treated with absolute devotion."

Elena Rossi San Francisco Chronicle Chef of the Year 2023

Our Story

At Savoria, we believe that exceptional dining begins with exceptional ingredients and unwavering dedication to craft.

Founded in 2018 in San Francisco's historic Montgomery Quarter, our restaurant has earned recognition as a Michelin-selected establishment, celebrating the marriage of classical French techniques with California's abundant coastal offerings.

Our commitment to sustainability drives every decision we make, from our partnerships with regenerative farms to our house-made preparations that honor time-tested traditions.
